Our Heritage

Crafting Arabian
Masterpieces

Located in vibrant Kaloor, Kochi, AL ARBANIA is a dedicated celebration of authentic Arabian culinary traditions. We bring to life the slow-roasted, tender perfection of Mandi, the smoky aroma of charcoal-fired Alfahm, juicy rotisserie Shawai, and freshly assembled shawarmas.

Every dish is crafted with passion using hand-picked spices, time-tested smoke infusing methods, and authentic Arabian hospitality. Come hungry, leave mesmerized.
